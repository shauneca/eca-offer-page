The framework

The Clarity Compass.
Four ingredients. One niche.

Most coaches pick a niche backwards. They chase what sounds good online and abandon it 6 weeks later. The Clarity Compass works the other way. It aligns four ingredients until your niche is so clear, nobody can really compete with you on it.

P / 01

Passion

What you genuinely care about helping people solve. The thing that keeps you showing up when the algorithm punishes you.

P / 02

Practice

What you've already solved for yourself or others. The receipts behind your message. The bit that makes it believable.

P / 03

Pain

A problem people want gone NOW, not someday. People don't pay thousands for growth. They pay to escape pain.

P / 04

Profit

Something people already pay real money to fix. If nobody's paying to solve this, you don't have a niche, you have a hobby.

Four answers. One sentence. The niche statement that does the selling for you.
