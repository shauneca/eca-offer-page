Honest answers

You're going to ask.
Here are the answers.

How fast will I see results? +

Most clients close their first £3-5k client within the first 60 days. Hitting £10k+ months happens before month 4 if they execute. Speed depends entirely on you, how fast you implement, how willing you are to do the inner work, how many hours you put in.

Do I need to be making money already? +

No. We work with coaches at £0/m and coaches at £5k/m. The method works at both ends. What matters is you have an offer (or are willing to define one in week 1) and you're ready to follow a system.

I've been burnt by coaching programmes before. Why is this different? +

You probably joined a programme that sold you tactics with no identity work. Or one with junior coaches you never spoke to the founder. Or one with no real system, just vibes and motivation. ECA isn't that. You get me on Slack daily, a structured 5-phase method, real accountability, and a dashboard that tracks whether the system is actually working for you.

How much time per week? +

Minimum 5 hours of focused implementation. Calls are 8 per month. Slack you can engage with on your own rhythm. The work is the work. If you can't give it 5 hours a week, this isn't the time.

What if I can't afford it? +

Pay As You Earn at £1,100/month was built for exactly this. Most coaches make their first payment back in their first month. If even £1,100 is impossible right now, you're probably not ready, focus on getting your first 1-2 clients, then come back.

Can I really DM you directly? +

Yes. Daily. The promise of founder access is the part most programmes lie about. I'm in Slack every day responding to clients. Ask any current ECA member.
